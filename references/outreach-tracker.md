# Outreach Tracker

Simple contact management for all outreach. Update as you send messages and get replies.

**Status Meanings:**
- `Sent` — Message sent, waiting for reply
- `Replied` — They responded
- `Interested` — They're interested, next step is conversation
- `Hot` — Ready to book a call or make a deal
- `Closed` — You signed them / they're a client
- `Not Interested` — They said no, thanks
- `Archived` — 10+ days, no response, moved on
