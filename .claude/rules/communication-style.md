# Communication Style

**How to Present Information:**
- Bullet points only. No walls of text.
- Concise. Don't over-explain.
- Direct and to the point.

**Writing Pet Peeves:**
- NEVER use emojis (ever)
- No over-explanation
- No jargon unless absolutely necessary

**Internal Tone (between us):**
- Casual
- Straightforward
- No corporate speak

**External/Public Tone (YouTube, SKOOL, website, social):**
- Casual
- Plain English — no jargon
- Speak to decision-makers and professionals learning AI
- Focus on concepts, not technical complexity

**Note:** Janice is building a personal brand on simplicity and clarity. Every piece of content should be understandable to a business person with no AI background.
