# Outreach System

**Purpose:** Enable you to reach out to small business owners and learning professionals WITHOUT feeling like you're doing outreach. The system generates personalized messages, tracks contacts, and reports only what matters. You spend 10 minutes per week. Max.

**Core Principle:** Outreach that feels invisible. Clients find you, not the other way around.

**When to use:**
- Weekly outreach session (pick one day, 10 minutes)
- When you have a list of leads and need messages
- When following up with interested prospects
- When reviewing who responded and who's hot

---

## Weekly Outreach Session (10 minutes)

**Monday morning or Friday afternoon — pick one**

1. Do you have a list of leads? (Copy-paste from LinkedIn, spreadsheet, whatever)
2. What audience are they? (Small business owners OR learning professionals)
3. What's the value-first angle? (Time savings, staying ahead of competition, learning, etc.)

I'll generate a batch of personalized messages you can review and send.

---

## How It Works

### Step 1: Prepare Your Leads

Get a list with these columns (minimal):
- Name
- Business (what they do)
- One observation (job title, something about their business, a problem you see they have)

Example:
```
Sarah, Dentist, struggles with patient scheduling (uses outdated system)
Marcus, E-commerce, probably overwhelmed with content creation
Jennifer, Insurance broker, similar problems to James
```

### Step 2: Generate Messages

Say: "Generate outreach messages for these leads: [paste list] | Audience: Small biz owners | Angle: Save time with AI automation"

I'll create:
- Personalized message for each lead (casual, plain English, 2-3 sentences max)
- Value-first (teach them something, show you understand their problem)
- Zero pressure (no "hire me" energy)
- Different version for follow-ups

### Step 3: Send (You choose how)

Option A: Copy-paste into LinkedIn DMs (manual, fastest for small batches)
Option B: Batch export to email template (do 10-20 at once)
Option C: Build JavaScript workflow (future build) — fully automated LinkedIn/email integration

### Step 4: Track

Add each contact to @references/outreach-tracker.md as you send.

Statuses:
- `Sent` → sent the message, waiting
- `Replied` → they responded
- `Interested` → they're interested in learning more
- `Hot` → they want to book a call
- `Closed` → you made a deal
- `Not Interested` → they said no
- `Archived` → 10+ days, no response, move on

### Step 5: Follow-Up

Day 4 after initial message:
- Say: "Generate follow-up 1 for these leads: [list of people who didn't reply yet]"
- I'll create different-angle follow-ups

Day 10:
- Say: "Generate final follow-up for these leads: [remaining silent ones]"
- This is the "closing the loop" message
- After this: archive them. Never spam.

---

## Message Principles

Every message you send should:
- Have value first (teach, help, or show you understand their problem)
- Be casual and plain English (no jargon, no sales-speak)
- Be 2-3 sentences max
- Mention ONE thing (lead gen, content creation, scheduling, whatever)
- End with a soft call to action ("curious if this resonates?" "Open to a quick chat?")
- Feel like you're helping, not selling
