# Follow-Up Template — Final Follow-Up (Day 10)

**Use this:** 10 days after initial message if still no reply

**Tone:** Casual, "closing the loop," low pressure, graceful

**Length:** 2 sentences max

**Goal:** Give them one last easy way to say yes, then move on

---

## Template

"Hey [Name], last message from me — I'll stop after this. If [thing you mentioned] sounds interesting even a little, just let me know. Otherwise, no hard feelings. Good luck with [their work]."

---

## Variations

### Friendly & Open
"Hey [Name], I'll let you get back to it. If you ever want to explore [automation] or chat about [topic], I'm here. Cheers!"

### Give Them an Easy Out
"Hey [Name], totally get it if this isn't your priority right now. If things change or you want to circle back, just holler. Good luck with [their business]."

### Leave a Door Open
"Hey [Name], I'll stop pestering you now. But if [situation changes] or you decide to explore [automation], you know where to find me."

### For Learning Audience
"Hey [Name], just wanted to say — if you decide to level up on AI and want to talk through it, I'm available. Otherwise, good luck with everything. You're doing great stuff."

---

## Rules

- Make it easy for them to say "yes" or "no"
- Don't guilt trip them for not responding
- Wish them well (genuinely)
- No emojis
- Keep it SHORT
- After this: ARCHIVE them. Do not send a 4th message. Ever.

---

## Archive Rules

After you send this message:
- Update their status to `Archived` in @references/outreach-tracker.md
