# LinkedIn Connection Request Template

**Use this:** Before sending an outreach message, connect first

**Tone:** Casual, specific, helpful

**Length:** 50 characters max for note (LinkedIn's limit)

**Goal:** Get them to accept the request so they see your message

---

## Templates

### Generic Version
"Hey [Name], saw your post on [topic]. Would love to connect."

### Specific Interest
"Noticed you're interested in [specific thing]. I build that. Would love to connect."

### Industry-Specific
"[Name], we should connect. You're doing interesting stuff with [their work]."

### For Mortgage / Finance
"Saw you're in [sector]. I help with [automation]. Let's connect."

### For Entrepreneurs
"Looking to connect with founders building [what they do]. Let's chat."

---

## Rules

- Be specific (reference something they posted or do)
- Keep it short (LinkedIn note limit is tight)
- Don't ask for anything in the connection request
- Only then: Follow up with the actual outreach message
- Wait 1-2 days after connection acceptance before sending outreach

---

## After They Accept

After they accept the connection (usually 1-2 hours to 1 day):

Send your cold outreach message in a DM. They'll see it because you're now connected.

Example flow:
1. Connection request: "Saw your post on lead gen. Would love to connect."
2. Wait 12-24 hours for acceptance
3. DM: "Hey [Name], I noticed you run [business]. A lot of [industry] teams are automating [process]..."
