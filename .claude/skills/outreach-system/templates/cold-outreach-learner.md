# Cold Outreach Template — Learning Professionals

**Audience:** Professionals considering pivoting into AI or wanting to understand AI better

**Tone:** Casual, encouraging, peer-to-peer, not salesy

**Length:** 2-3 sentences max

**Structure:**
1. Acknowledge what they're interested in (1 sentence)
2. Show what they might be missing (1 sentence)
3. Soft offer (1 sentence)

---

## Template

"Hey [Name], I see you're interested in [AI topic]. Most professionals learning this in 2026 are building hands-on systems, not just reading. I teach people how to think in AI so they can actually direct it. Want to chat about it?"

---

## Variations

### For Career Pivoters
"Hey [Name], looks like you're exploring AI. A lot of professionals coming from [background] are building real projects while they learn — not just courses. I help with the building part. Open to a quick chat?"

### For Business Decision-Makers
"Hey [Name], your company probably needs AI literacy at leadership level. I teach execs how AI actually works so they make better decisions. Might be worth a 15-min call?"

### For Entrepreneurs
"Hey [Name], entrepreneurs using AI are 10x faster than their competitors now. I teach the practical stuff — how to prompt, how to automate, how to think through builds. Curious if that's interesting?"

### For Content Creators
"Hey [Name], creators who understand AI are automating their content pipeline. I show people how to do that without becoming engineers. Worth exploring?"

### For Job Seekers in AI
"Hey [Name], I see you're pursuing AI. A lot of people breaking into the field build real projects first — makes hiring way easier. I help with that build process. Chat?"

---

## Rules

- Don't assume they want to buy
- Assume they want to LEARN or GROW
- Reference what they're interested in (posts, profile, something specific)
- Mention YOUR angle: teaching, hands-on, "thinking in AI"
- Ask a question (no pressure)
- Keep it under 3 sentences
- No emojis
- No salesy language
