# Follow-Up Template — 1st Follow-Up (Day 4)

**Use this:** 4 days after the initial message if you get no reply

**Tone:** Casual, different angle, still value-first

**Length:** 2 sentences max

**Goal:** Give them a new reason to respond without being pushy

---

## Template

"Hey [Name], just want to make sure you saw my last message about [specific thing]. Thought of something that might apply to you — [new angle or new example]. Open to it?"

---

## Variations by Context

### If They Seemed Interested in Lead Gen
"Hey [Name], following up on the lead gen thing I mentioned. Just helped someone in [similar industry] save 6 hours a week on qualification. Does your team have that pain point?"

### If They Seemed Interested in Content
"Hey [Name], re: the content automation piece. A lot of [industry] creators are batching content on Sunday and auto-scheduling it. Saves them the daily grind. Sound useful?"

### If They Seemed Interested in Learning
"Hey [Name], quick follow-up — I'm running a cohort soon on hands-on AI building. Thought of you because of [specific reason]. Still interested in chatting?"

### If They Mentioned a Specific Problem
"Hey [Name], I mentioned [automation], but thinking about what you said about [their problem], there's another angle that might work better. Worth a quick call?"

---

## Rules

- Reference the initial message (show continuity)
- Introduce a NEW angle or example (not repeating)
- Keep it brief (don't oversell)
- Ask an easy question at the end
- No emojis
- No "I notice you didn't reply" (makes them defensive)
