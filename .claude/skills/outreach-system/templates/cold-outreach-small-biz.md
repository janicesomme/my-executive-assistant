# Cold Outreach Template — Small Business Owners

**Audience:** Small business owners who need specific process automation

**Tone:** Casual, helpful, value-first, zero pressure

**Length:** 2-3 sentences max

**Structure:**
1. Show you understand their world (1 sentence)
2. Mention one specific pain point you solve (1 sentence)
3. Soft ask (1 sentence)

---

## Template

"Hey [Name], I noticed you run [business type]. A lot of [business types] are automated [specific process] now and saving hours every week. Open to a quick conversation about how?"

---

## Variations by Industry

### For Lead Gen / Sales Teams
"Hey [Name], I build lead gen systems for [industry]. Most teams I talk to spend 10+ hours/week on lead qualification manually. Have you automated that yet?"

### For Content Creators / Marketing
"Hey [Name], I've been helping [industry] teams automate their [specific content type] creation. Saves them about [X] hours a week. Worth a chat?"

### For E-commerce / Operations
"Hey [Name], a lot of [industry] owners are using AI for [specific task]. I build those systems. Curious if that's on your radar?"

### For Mortgage / Finance
"Hey [Name], I noticed you're in [specific type]. The lenders I work with are using AI to [specific automation]. Changed their whole operation. Open to exploring?"

### For Service Businesses
"Hey [Name], most [service type] businesses lose revenue to manual [specific process]. I automate that. Might be worth 15 min to see if it applies to you?"

---

## Rules

- Use their name
- Reference their actual business (show you looked)
- Mention ONE specific thing you automate (not "everything")
- Ask a question at the end (not a demand)
- Keep it under 3 sentences
- No emojis
- No "I'm launching" or "hire me" energy
