# Focus/Accountability System

**Purpose:** Keep Janice focused on ONE thing per day. Prevent mid-day pivots to unrelated work. Track progress and finish what she starts.

**When to use:**
- Start of day (daily standup)
- Mid-day check-in (when feeling unfocused)
- End of week (weekly review)
- Anytime she feels the shiny object syndrome pulling her away

---

## Daily Standup (Start of Day)

Ask:
1. What's your ONE focus today? (Pick from current-priorities.md)
2. What specific outcome are you shipping today?
3. What would distract you from this? (List the shiny objects)
4. What will you NOT do today?

Then: "Alright, your focus is [X]. Protect that. Everything else is a distraction."

---

## Mid-Day Check-In (Use when losing focus)

Ask:
1. What were you supposed to be doing?
2. What are you actually doing?
3. Is the new thing urgent? (Does it move mortgage lender system forward? SKOOL community? YouTube? No? Then it's a distraction.)
4. What do you need to get back on track?

---

## Weekly Review (Friday or End of Week)

Ask:
1. What was your focus for the week? (3-5 items)
2. What did you ship? (Be specific — finished code, written videos, modules, etc.)
3. What didn't you finish? Why?
4. Did shiny object syndrome hit? When?
5. Next week's focus: What's the #1 thing?

Log to @decisions/log.md: [DATE] FOCUS REVIEW: [What you did] | [What didn't finish] | [Shiny objects encountered] | [Next focus]

---

## Anti-Shiny Object Rules

When something new pops up, ask:
- Does this move me toward revenue? (mortgage lender, SKOOL, YouTube, content org)
- Is it urgent (deadline this week)?
- Can it wait until [specific date]?

If not urgent: "Add it to the backlog. Don't context-switch."

---

## Success Metric

80% task completion rate. If you're finishing 80% of what you commit to each week, the system is working.

---

**Invoke this skill:** Use daily standup in the morning. Mid-day check-in when you feel scattered. Weekly review on Friday.
